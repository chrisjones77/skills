# The New Print Shop for DOS

Use the IBM PC/Tandy product pictured in Brøderbund’s catalogue. For a sign, study the “Hilary’s Room” example on printed p. 3. Set the short display lines independently, choose the scale of the small graphic stamps, then assemble a repeat border. The specimen leaves paper around the message. For a card, the catalogue gives other balances of type and illustration. Let a short greeting support a larger graphic when appropriate.

For a flyer or notice, keep the approved reading order in the main slot. The starter uses a repeat diamond border, centred message blocks and a single replaceable stamp. Break display copy only at a natural phrase, and keep the exact words. Use the bundled Paper Grid Serif faces for every printed role. Their proportional letterforms were drawn from a one-bit strike of a licensed modern serif. At large sizes the steps around the bowl of a B or the diagonal of an A should be visible even on a plain white page. The PDF embeds the stepped contours so the words stay selectable. Vary face or scale by line when the specimen calls for it. Draw fresh stamp artwork for the subject. Preview the composition in plain ink first. A requested nine-pin colour pass belongs to the printing treatment and should preserve the clean editable master.

For a folded card, assign each block to front, inside-left, inside-right or back. For a banner, set assembly width_mm, height_mm and overlap_mm, and leave room for the guide strip. Use the generated fold and tile instructions.
