# Tandy DeskMate

## Draw flyers and notices

For a request such as “DeskMate Draw poster”, prepare a `deskmate` flyer. The notice also uses the Draw starter. Pick a specific manual example before composing. On printed p. 49, the field-trip announcement places outline dinosaurs beside a short centred message. On p. 45 the birthday card uses an outlined cake, balloons and a zigzag. Borrow the construction and draw the subject the brief calls for. The two balloons in the starter are editable placeholders.

Place each title line separately as in the card tutorial. It selects Roman, medium and centred. Liberation Serif stands in for Roman. Change line size to fit the paper and leave space between the drawings and lettering. Draw one recognisable object using ellipses, straight segments and a small number of connected brush strokes. Use a flat colour or a repeating fill in selected closed areas. Preserve open paper. Compare the result at page size with the chosen manual example. A pixelated landscape, desktop window or checklist icon does not establish a Draw treatment.

## Office documents

Letters and memos use the sheet template. Forms use ruled writing fields and compact labels. The six form rows have three columns with source-controlled headings. The slots are main, name, contact, offering and footer. Other DeskMate templates use main. Preserve copy, allow room for handwriting and keep body text at least 10 pt. The office typography and the default print palette are production choices.
