# IBM Selectric

Use one element throughout. This starter chooses a 10-character-per-inch pica pitch and six lines per inch. Approximate the character advance with 12 pt Liberation Mono. Underline the subject, leave a blank line between sections and keep a ragged right margin. A dramatic heading comes from its placement on the sheet.

Select `selectric` for flyer, notice, letter, memo. Place the copy in the main slot.
