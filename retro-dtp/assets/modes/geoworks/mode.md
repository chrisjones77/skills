# GeoWorks Ensemble for DOS

Choose the application by the work. GeoWrite sets flowing text in scalable type. For a newsletter, decide the column width and gutter from the reading order. Robert Bixby’s 1992 chapbook account describes arranging two landscape columns for folding. That is a useful precedent when the job actually folds. Letterhead and short notices can use one clear text area.

For a poster, use the GeoDraw flyer starter. Begin with the festival sheet pictured in the March 1991 advertisement. Place a small spot drawing to the left of a ruled masthead and give the title more weight than the event details. Keep the graphic and type independently movable. Replace the sample graphic with the subject. Reserve rotation, overlap and grouping for objects that benefit from them. Check that curves and lettering remain clean at print size. A sidebar is optional. Liberation Serif and Sans are modern scalable substitutes. The 1.2 release announcement documents these drawing and typesetting capabilities. The starter is our own composition.

The newsletter starter has two explicit sheets. Slots are masthead, left1, right1, sidebar, footer1, head2, left2, right2 and footer2, in that semantic order. Keep block order equal to that order. For one, three or four pages, edit the prepared HTML: remove unused sheets or add a sheet and move existing block bindings into bounded regions. Update footer text in document.json and the approved page_limit. Do not duplicate a binding or shrink text to hide overflow. A title-only page limit change does not reflow the layout. If copy still does not fit, ask for a content or page decision.
