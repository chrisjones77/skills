# Privacy and sharing

- Treat manifests, prompts, reviewer notes, and asset metadata as potentially sensitive project data.
- Keep source images and private references in access-controlled storage. Prefer opaque evidence references over embedding private paths or personal details.
- Use synthetic, clearly marked fixtures for examples and tests; never mistake fixture assertions for observed evidence.
- Before sharing a manifest or asset, have the responsible owner review permissions, privacy, attribution, and any embedded names or claims.
- SHA-256 establishes byte identity only; it does not establish ownership, consent, truth, or approval.

This guidance supports careful handling; it does not determine rights or authorize publication.
