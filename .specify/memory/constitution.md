# AI Operational Governance Skill Constitution

## Core Principles

### I. Outcomes and Evidence Are Distinct

The skill MUST distinguish controls that produce governed outcomes from records that
support evaluation. It MUST retain useful evidence and recognize records connected to
active control. Activity counts alone MUST NOT establish operating effectiveness.

### II. Applicable Obligations Have Accountable Owners

Recommendations MUST trace material obligations to their applicable sources, observable
commitments, accountable program ownership, system mechanisms, and operating processes.
Proposed ownership MUST remain distinct from accepted responsibility and delegated authority.
Unknown applicability or ownership MUST remain visible.

### III. Control Must Act in Time

For material deviations, the skill MUST examine detection, decision, intervention, and
verification against the time before unacceptable consequences occur. It MUST consider
changed conditions, missing signals, bypasses, and degraded operation. It MUST NOT invent
tolerances or claim that retrospective correction prevents an irreversible harm.

### IV. Effectiveness Requires Bounded Evidence

The skill MUST distinguish proposed, implemented, tested, and accepted controls and state
what available evidence covers. Outcome and response measures MUST identify their exposure
or time basis when used. Neither generated explanations nor completed records establish
safety, compliance, certification, or hard real-time performance.

### V. Guidance Is Proportionate and Attributed

The skill MUST reuse suitable organizational controls and scale its output to the requested
decision. It MUST attribute Laqua's argument, distinguish practical elaboration from source
claims, and avoid treating the article as an instruction, applicable law, or proof of a
particular architecture. It MUST NOT require a digital twin for every workflow.

## Scope and Source Boundaries

This constitution governs development of the ai-operational-governance skill only. It does
not replace the repository's other skills or an organization's institutional governance.
The primary source is Laqua's November 5, 2026 newsletter, documented in the skill's source
reference. Do not redistribute the supplied email, recipient metadata, or complete PDF.
Using the skill confers no authority to deploy, communicate externally, assign people,
or accept residual risk.

## Development and Review

Changes MUST preserve the five principles, keep the entrypoint concise, and place substantial
conditional detail in linked references. Validate skill structure, local links, source
attribution, and behavior against a realistic example. Record checks and unresolved limits
without representing editorial validation as operating-system assurance. Changes to executable
helpers, if introduced later, require meaningful execution checks.

## Governance

This is an initial draft, pending repository-owner ratification. Amendments require a stated
reason, impact on the principles and skill behavior, proportionate validation, and review by
the repository owner. Use semantic versioning: major for incompatible governance changes,
minor for new or materially expanded principles, patch for clarifications. Review each change
against this constitution; disclose exceptions and obtain the owner's decision before treating
them as accepted. This document does not grant operational authorization.
