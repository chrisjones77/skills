# Soil, drainage and layout

Map slopes, low points, runoff paths and persistent standing water. Record where and how long water stands after rain. Note texture, surface crusting and compaction separately. Compare repeated observations at similar rainfall, and record method and depth for any soil test.

Compare small reversible trials before earthworks. Record effects on access, drainage at bed edges and adjacent land. For rows and paths, check sun, slope, runoff and access together. For greenhouse siting, record sun, wind exposure, drainage, access, anchorage and snow-load assumptions. Treat structural or regulatory checks as a separate decision and retain any open verification in the plan.
