# Recipe provenance

Retain the original separately, with its source, date if known, and sharing permission. Mark illegible or uncertain text in a faithful transcription. Record corrections or normalizations as a separate version with a link back to the transcription.

For each proposed adaptation, identify the version, author, date, change and reason. Compare preservation-relevant differences with an applicable tested recipe using the [delta register](../templates/recipe-delta-register.md). Keep provenance and process status separate. Use synthetic examples in public work unless the record owner authorizes publication.
