# Shelf-stable preservation gate

Use a current authoritative tested recipe applicable to the food and the exact intended method. Check ingredients and quantities, acidity, thickener, packing method, jar size, headspace, altitude adjustment and processing. Record the authoritative source with the specific recipe and review date. [Health Canada home canning safety](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/general-food-safety-tips/home-canning-safety.html)

- **PASS** applies only when the intended recipe and process match the source or a variation the source explicitly permits.
- **HOLD** applies when information needed to make that comparison is missing. Obtain it before proceeding.
- **BLOCK** applies when a known change lacks validation. Record the difference and stop the shelf-stable recommendation.

Treat sealed jars, prior success and household ratio calculations as insufficient evidence. For a proposed unsupported change, return to the exact tested recipe or consider a separately supported refrigerated or frozen preparation. A tested recipe for a different product provides no validation for the change.
