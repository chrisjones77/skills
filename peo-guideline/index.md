# PEO Professional Engineering Practice Guideline Skill

This skill provides reference to the Professional Engineers Ontario (PEO) Professional Engineering Practice Guideline (November 2020).

## What This Covers

- **Licensing**: Licence classifications, consulting engineer designation, use of titles
- **Professional Responsibility**: Competence, duty of care, professional conduct
- **Engineer's Duty to Report**: Safety warnings, incompetence/misconduct reporting
- **Rules of Practice**: Seal usage, client relations, due diligence, report writing, communications, document retention, confidentiality
- **Conflicts of Interest**: Recognition, disclosure, case examples
- **Certificate of Authorization**: Requirements for offering services to the public
- **Advertising**: Professional standards for practitioner advertising
- **Code of Ethics**: Principles and rules governing professional conduct

## How to Use

Invoke this skill with `/peo-guideline` when you need guidance on:
- Professional engineering practice standards in Ontario
- Ethical obligations and professional conduct
- Licensing requirements and restrictions
- Conflict of interest situations
- Client/employer relationship guidelines
- Report writing and documentation standards
- Duty to report safety concerns or misconduct

## Source

Professional Engineers Ontario, "Professional Engineering Practice Guideline", November 2020
